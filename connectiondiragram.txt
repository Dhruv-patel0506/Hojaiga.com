CONNECTION VISUALIZATION LAW

After successfully creating and integrating EACH individual organ/file, create one connection-state diagram showing how the new component connects to the previously completed component.

The diagram MUST visually represent the components as independent physical modules being connected progressively.

LEFT SIDE:
Show the PREVIOUSLY COMPLETED component as a simple hand-drawn/pencil-style technical sketch. Do NOT show its entire architecture or workflow. Represent it only as its physical/module form, with its pipeline wire visibly extending outward and remaining UNCONNECTED/LOOSE.

RIGHT SIDE:
Show the NEWLY CREATED component as a separate hand-drawn/pencil-style technical sketch. Do NOT show its entire architecture or workflow. Represent only its physical/module form, with its corresponding SOCKET clearly visible and ready to receive the wire.

BETWEEN THEM:
Show the loose pipeline/wire from the previous component approaching the new component's socket.

After successful integration, show the wire correctly inserted into the socket and clearly indicate that the connection is COMPLETE.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a workflow diagram.
This is NOT a system architecture diagram.
This is NOT an AI-generated realistic image.
It is a simple pencil/technical-sketch representation of two independent modules physically connecting through:

PREVIOUS ORGAN → PIPELINE/WIRE → SOCKET → NEW ORGAN

The purpose is to visually document the progressive construction of the system, where every organ remains independently built and is connected to the growing system one connection at a time.

Keep the same visual convention for every subsequent organ so that all connection diagrams form a consistent historical record of the system being assembled.
