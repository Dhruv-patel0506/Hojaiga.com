# MIT OpenCourseWare  (ocw.mit.edu)

MIT OpenCourseWare is a web based publication of virtually all MIT course content. OCW is open and available to the world and is a permanent MIT activity

## How to use it

- MIT OpenCourseWare: MIT OpenCourseWare is a web based publication of virtually all MIT course content. OCW is open and available to the world and is a permanent MIT activity
- Main navigation: menu, Give Now, About OCW, Help & Faqs, Contact Us.
- There is a search box on the page: that is the fastest way in.
- Menus / buttons that open something: Search, RESOURCES, show compact results, ABOUT OCW, Explore, Get Started, Open feedback survey, Educators Start Here.
- Kinds of pages (from its sitemap): /courses (11), /newsletter (1), /about (1), /educator (1), /external-resources (1), /milestones (1).
