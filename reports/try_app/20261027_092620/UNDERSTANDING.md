# Bandcamp  (bandcamp.com)

Discover amazing music and directly support the artists who make it.

## How to use it

- Bandcamp: Discover amazing music and directly support the artists who make it.
- Main navigation: Bandcamp home, Search, Sign up, Log in, Digital music, Vinyl, Compact discs, Cassettes, T-shirts, Gift cards.
- There is a search box on the page: that is the fastest way in.
- Menus / buttons that open something: Search for artist, album or track Search, Bandcamp home, Log in, Digital music, Vinyl, Compact discs, Cassettes, T-shirts.
