# Our World in Data  (ourworldindata.org)

Research and data to make progress against the world’s largest problems.

## How to use it

- Our World in Data: Research and data to make progress against the world’s largest problems.
- Main navigation: Toggle menu, Our World in Data, Browse by topic, Data, Latest, Resources, About, Subscribe, Donate.
- There is a search box on the page: that is the fastest way in.
- Menus / buttons that open something: Table, Map, Line, Show more chart types, Chart settings, Life expectancy Long-run estimates collated from multiple so, CO₂ emissions per capita Long-run estimates from the Global , GDP per capita Long-run estimates from the Maddison Project .
- Kinds of pages (from its sitemap): /grapher (1456), /data-insights (466), /sdgs (18), /featured-viz (4), /explorers (1), /search (1).
