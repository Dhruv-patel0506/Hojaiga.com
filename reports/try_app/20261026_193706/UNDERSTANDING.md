# lichess.org  (lichess.org)

Free online chess server. Play chess in a clean interface. No registration, no ads, no plugin required. Play chess with the computer, friends or random opponents.

## How to use it

- lichess.org: Free online chess server. Play chess in a clean interface. No registration, no ads, no plugin required. Play chess with the computer, friends or random opponents.
- Main navigation: lichess.org, PLAY, Donate, PUZZLES, LEARN, WATCH, COMMUNITY, TOOLS, DONATE, SIGN IN.
- There is a search box on the page: that is the fastest way in.
- Menus / buttons that open something: Quick pairing, Lobby, Correspondence, Accessibility - Enable bli, lichess.org, PLAY, PUZZLES, Disable blind mode.
