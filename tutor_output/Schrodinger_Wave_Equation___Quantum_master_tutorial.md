### TIER 1: MULTIMODAL PERCEPTION & DIAGRAM DECONSTRUCTION

**Schrodinger Wave Equation:**
- **Symbolism:** The Schrodinger Wave Equation is represented by the symbol \(\hat{\psi}\), which represents the wave function of a quantum system.
- **Formulas:** The equation is given by:
  \[
  \hat{\psi}(x, t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \hat{\psi}(x', t) e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}(x - x')t} dx'
  \]
  where \(\hat{\psi}(x, t)\) is the wave function, \(\hbar\) is the reduced Planck constant, and \(i\) is the imaginary unit.
- **Axes and Schematic Components:**
  - **Time Axis (t):** Represents the evolution of the wave function over time.
  - **Spatial Axis (x):** Represents the position of the particle.
  - **Wave Function (\(\hat{\psi}\)):** Represents the probability amplitude of finding the particle at position \(x\) at time \(t\).
  - **Probability Density (\(\hat{\psi}^*\hat{\psi}\)):** Represents the probability of finding the particle at position \(x\) at time \(t\).

**Quantum Tunneling:**
- **Symbolism:** Quantum tunneling is represented by the symbol \(e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}kx}\), where \(k\) is the wave number.
- **Formulas:** The probability amplitude for tunneling through a potential barrier is given by:
  \[
  A(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \hat{\psi}(x', t) e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}(x - x')t} dx'
  \]
  where \(x\) is the position of the particle.
- **Axes and Schematic Components:**
  - **Time Axis (t):** Represents the evolution of the wave function over time.

### TIER 2: FIRST-PRINCIPLES INTUITION & MENTAL MODELS

**Schrodinger Wave Equation:**
- **Intuition:** The Schrodinger Wave Equation is a fundamental equation in quantum mechanics that describes the behavior of quantum systems. It is a wave equation that governs the evolution of the wave function of a quantum system over time.
- **Real-World Analogies:** Think of the Schrodinger Wave Equation as the "recipe" for a quantum system. Just as a recipe tells you how to make a dish, the Schrodinger Wave Equation tells you how to evolve the wave function of a quantum system over time.

**Quantum Tunneling:**
- **Intuition:** Quantum tunneling is a phenomenon where a particle can pass through a potential barrier that it classically cannot cross. This is a counterintuitive effect that arises from the probabilistic nature of quantum mechanics.
- **Real-World Analogies:** Imagine a particle that is supposed to be trapped in a potential well. Quantum tunneling allows the particle to pass through the potential barrier and continue on its way, even though it would not be able to do so classically.

### TIER 5: SOCRATIC PRACTICE DRILLS & WORKED SOLUTIONS

**Schrodinger Wave Equation:**
- **Beginner Problem:** A particle is in a one-dimensional infinite potential well of width \(L\). Find the wave function and the probability density for the particle.
  - **Solution:** The wave function is given by:
    \[
    \hat{\psi}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{L}} \sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right)
    \]
    The probability density is given by:
    \[
    \hat{\psi}^*\hat{\psi} = \frac{2}{L} \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right)
    \]
- **Intermediate Problem:** A particle is in a one-dimensional infinite potential well of width \(L\). Find the probability that the particle is found in the region \(0 \leq x \leq \frac{L}{2}\).
  - **Solution:** The probability is given by:
    \[
    P = \int_0^{\frac{L}{2}} \frac{2}{L} \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right) dx = \frac{1}{2}
    \]
- **Master Problem:** A particle is in a one-dimensional infinite potential well of width \(L\). Find the probability that the particle is found in the region \(0 \leq x \leq \frac{L}{2}\) and \(L/2 \leq x \leq L\).
  - **Solution:** The probability is given by:
    \[
    P = \int_0^{\frac{L}{2}} \frac{2}{L} \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right) dx + \int_{\frac{L}{2}}^{L} \frac{2}{L} \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right) dx = \frac{1}{2}
    \]

**Quantum Tunneling:**
