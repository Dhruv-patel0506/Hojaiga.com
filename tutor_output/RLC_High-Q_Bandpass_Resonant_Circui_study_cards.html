RLC High-Q Bandpass Resonant Circuit & Phase Bode Plot

### TIER 1: MULTIMODAL PERCEPTION & DIAGRAM DECONSTRUCTION

**RLC High-Q Bandpass Resonant Circuit:**
- **Circuit Components:**
  - **Resistor (R):** Acts as a load for the circuit.
  - **Inductor (L):** Stores energy in its magnetic field.
  - **Capacitor (C):** Stores energy in its electric field.
  - **Voltage Source (V):** Provides the energy for the circuit.

**Schematic Diagram:**
```
V --- R --- L --- C --- GND
```
- **Axes:**
  - **Real Axis (R):** Represents the real part of the impedance.
  - **Imaginary Axis (I):** Represents the imaginary part of the impedance.

**Phase Bode Plot:**
- **Frequency Axis (F):** Represents the frequency of the input signal.
- **Magnitude Axis (M):** Represents the magnitude of the output signal.
- **Phase Axis (P):** Represents the phase shift between the input and output signals.

**Vector Arrows:**
- **Impedance Vector (Z):** Represents the impedance of the circuit at a given frequency.
- **Magnitude Vector (|Z|):** Represents the magnitude of the impedance.
- **Phase Vector (∠Z):** Represents the phase angle of the impedance.

### TIER 2: FIRST-PRINCIPLES INTUITION & MENTAL MODELS

**Why This Concept Exists:**
- **RLC Circuit Behavior:**
  - **Resistor (R):** Acts as a load, dissipating energy.
  - **Inductor (L):** Stores energy in its magnetic field, storing energy when current is increasing and releasing it when current is decreasing.
  - **Capacitor (C):** Stores energy in its electric field, storing energy when voltage is increasing and releasing it when voltage is decreasing.
- **Bandpass Resonant Circuit:**
  - **Bandpass Filter:**
    - Allows frequencies within a specific range to pass through while attenuating frequencies outside this range.
  - **High-Q Resonant Circuit:**
    - Has a high quality factor (Q), meaning it has a sharp resonance peak and a narrow bandwidth.

**Real-World Analogies:**
- **Resistor (R):** Acts as a load in a power plant, dissipating energy.
- **Inductor (L):** Stores energy in a power transmission line, storing energy when current is increasing and releasing it when current is decreasing.
- **Capacitor (C):** Stores energy in a power distribution network, storing energy when voltage is increasing and releasing it when voltage is decreasing.

### TIER 3: FORMAL STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION & PROOF

**Mathematical Derivation:**
1. **Impedance of RLC Circuit:**
   \[
   Z = R + j(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C})
   \]
   where \( \omega \) is the angular frequency, \( j \) is the imaginary unit, and \( \omega = 2\pi f \) is the frequency in radians per second.

2. **Magnitude of Impedance:**
   \[
   |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C})^2}
   \]

3. **Phase Angle of Impedance:**
   \[
   \angle Z = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}}{R}\right)
   \]

4. **Bandpass Filter Transfer Function:**
   \[
   H(j\omega) = \frac{1}{1 + j\omega RC}
   \]

5. **High-Q Resonant Circuit:**
   \[
   Q = \frac{\omega L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega C}
   \]

### TIER 4: COGNITIVE HURDLE DIAGNOSTIC

**Common Pitfalls:**
1. **Incorrect Impedance Calculation:**
   - **Error:** Failing to account for the phase angle in the impedance calculation.
   - **Solution:** Always include the phase angle in the impedance calculation using \( Z = R + j(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}) \).

2. **Incorrect Bandpass Filter Transfer Function:**
   - **Error:** Failing to account for the phase shift in the transfer function.
   - **Solution:** Always include the phase shift in the transfer function using \( H(j\omega) = \frac{1}{1 + j\omega RC} \).

3. **Incorrect High-Q Resonant Circuit Calculation:**
   - **Error:** Failing to account for the phase angle in the resonant circuit calculation.
   - **Solution:** Always include the phase angle in the resonant circuit calculation using \( Q = \frac{\omega L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega C} \).

### TIER 5: SOCRATIC PRACTICE DRILLS & WORKED SOLUTIONS

**Progressive Practice Problems:**

**Problem 1:**
- **RLC Circuit:**
  - \( R = 10 \Omega \), \( L = 0.1 \text{ H} \), \( C = 1 \text{ F} \), \( V = 10 \text{ V} \).
  - Calculate the impedance at \( \omega = 100 \text{ rad/s} \).

**Solution:**
1. **Impedance Calculation:**
   \[
   Z = R + j(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}) = 10 + j(100 \times 0.1 - \frac{1}{100 \times 1}) = 10 + j(10 - 0.01) = 10 + j9.99
   \]
2. **Magnitude Calculation:**
   \[
   |Z| = \sqrt{10^2 + 9.99^2} = \sqrt{100 + 99.8001} = \sqrt{199.8001} \approx 14.13 \Omega
   \]
3. **Phase Calculation:**
   \[
   \angle Z = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{9.99}{10}\right) \approx 44.5^\circ
   \]

**Problem 2:**
- **RLC Circuit:**
  - \( R = 20 \Omega \), \( L = 0.2 \text{ H} \), \( C = 0.5 \text{ F} \), \( V = 20 \text{ V} \).
  - Calculate the transfer function at \( \omega = 200 \text{ rad/s} \).

**Solution:**
1. **Transfer Function Calculation:**
   \[
   H(j\omega) = \frac{1}{1 + j\omega RC} = \frac{1}{1 + j200 \times 20 \times 0.5} = \frac{1}{1 + j200}
   \]
2. **Magnitude Calculation:**
   \[
   |H(j\omega)| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1^2 + 200^2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{40001}} \approx 0.025
   \]
3. **Phase Calculation:**
   \[
   \angle H(j\omega) = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{200}{1}\right) \approx 89.4^\circ
   \]

**Problem 3:**
- **RLC Circuit:**
  - \( R = 30 \Omega \), \( L = 0.3 \text{ H} \), \( C = 1.5 \text{ F} \), \( V = 30 \text{ V} \).
  - Calculate the resonant frequency and the quality factor.

**Solution:**
1. **Resonant Frequency Calculation:**
   \[
   \omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{0.3 \times 1.5}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{0.45}} \approx 1.55 \text{ rad/s}
   \]
2. **Quality Factor Calculation:**
   \[
   Q = \frac{\omega_0L}{R} = \frac{1.55 \times 0.3}{30} = \frac{0.465}{30} \approx 0.0155
   \]
