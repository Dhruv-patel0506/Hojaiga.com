### Components of a Free Body Diagram

A free body diagram is a visual representation used to analyze the forces acting on an object. It includes the object itself, labeled with its mass or weight, and all external forces acting on it, such as gravity, friction, and applied forces. The diagram helps in understanding the net force and acceleration of the object.

- **Explanation:** 1. Object: Represents the body being analyzed.
- **Explanation:** 2. Mass/Weight: Indicates the object's mass or weight.
- **Explanation:** 3. External Forces: Includes gravity, friction, and applied forces.
- **Explanation:** 4. Arrows: Represent the direction and magnitude of forces.
- **Explanation:** 5. Resultant Force: The net force acting on the object.
- **Explanation:** 6. Acceleration: The object's acceleration due to the net force.
