### Introduction to Free Body Diagrams

Free Body Diagrams are visual tools used to analyze the forces acting on an object. They help in understanding the net force and the direction of motion.

- **Explanation:** A Free Body Diagram shows all forces acting on an object.
- **Explanation:** Each force is represented by an arrow pointing in the direction of the force.
- **Explanation:** The net force is the vector sum of all forces.
- **Explanation:** This helps in determining the object's acceleration.
- **Explanation:** Understanding these diagrams is crucial for applying Newton's laws of motion.
