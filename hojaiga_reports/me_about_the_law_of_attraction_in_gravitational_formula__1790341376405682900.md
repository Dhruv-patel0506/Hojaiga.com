### Derivation of Gravitational Formula

The law of attraction in gravitational formula is derived from Newton's law of universal gravitation. This law states that every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

- **Explanation:** Newton's law of universal gravitation: F = G * (m1 * m2) / r^2
- **Explanation:** Where F is the force of attraction, G is the gravitational constant, m1 and m2 are the masses of the two objects, and r is the distance between their centers.
- **Explanation:** This formula quantifies the gravitational force between two objects, illustrating the inverse square relationship with distance.
- **Explanation:** The law of attraction in gravitational terms emphasizes the proportional relationship between mass and distance in gravitational force.
