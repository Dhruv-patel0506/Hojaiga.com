The law of attraction in gravitational force is explained through the formula F = G * (m1 * m2) / r^2, where F is the gravitational force, G is the gravitational constant, m1 and m2 are the masses of the two objects, and r is the distance between their centers. This formula quantifies the attractive force between two masses.
