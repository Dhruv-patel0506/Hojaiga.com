You can easily verify your VRAM (Video RAM) yourself in Windows in just a few seconds using either the Task Manager or your Display Settings. Here are the two easiest ways:

Method 1: Using Task Manager (Easiest)
Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc on your keyboard to open the Task Manager.
Click on the Performance tab on the left sidebar (it looks like a little graph/speedometer icon).
Scroll down on the left side and click on GPU 1 (usually labeled as your NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050).
Look at the bottom section under the graphs. You will see "Dedicated GPU memory". It will say something like x.x GB / 6.0 GB. That 6.0 GB is your VRAM!
Method 2: Using Windows Settings
Right-click anywhere on your desktop background and select Display settings.
Scroll down and click on Advanced display.
Click on Display adapter properties for Display 1.
A small window will pop up. Look for the line that says "Dedicated Video Memory". It will list your exact VRAM in MB (for a 6GB card, it will say 6144 MB).
